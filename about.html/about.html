Our Story

The Kingdom Culture Club was founded to create a supportive space where young individuals can express their gifts and passion for automotive culture while honoring God.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To honor God by creating a supportive, faith-driven community where young people use their talents, automotive passions, and creative skills to uplift one another.

Our Vision

To build a transformative hub that bridges faith, culture, and community involvement—empowering youth to walk in purpose.

Leadership Team

Our team is dedicated to organizing safe meets, mentorship workshops, and outreach projects.
